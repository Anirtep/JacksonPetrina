# /roast — Stress-test any idea before committing

When this skill is invoked, ask Jackson 3 questions before running the council:
1. Who is the actual target buyer or beneficiary?
2. What edge or resource does Jackson already have?
3. What are the constraints? (budget, timeline, how fast does this need to work?)

Then spin up 5 sub-agent perspectives and synthesize:

**Contrarian** — What is the single fatal flaw? Assume it will fail. What kills it?
**Expansionist** — What is the biggest possible upside if this succeeds beyond expectation?
**First Principles** — Strip away all assumptions. Using only pure logic, does this make sense?
**Deep Researcher** — What does the market data, competitor landscape, or prior art say?
**Buyer** — Role-playing as the actual end user/customer: would I pay for this? Why or why not?

**Judge's Verdict:**
- **Green Light** — ship as described
- **Reshape** — keep the core, change the approach (most common outcome)
- **Kill** — fatal structural flaw, don't build this

**Cheapest 48-hour test:** The single lowest-cost action Jackson can take right now to validate this before committing real resources.

Rules:
- Give the Contrarian and Buyer the most weight — they find what the creator's bias hides
- The Judge cannot give Green Light without at least one dissenting voice being heard — unchallenged approval from the same person who asked is worthless
- Always include the cheapest test, even if verdict is Green Light
