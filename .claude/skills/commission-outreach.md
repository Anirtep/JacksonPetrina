# /commission-outreach — DM strategy for reaching potential commission buyers

This skill is for when Jackson wants to reach out to a potential commission buyer —
collectors, conscious entrepreneurs, retreat organizers, or anyone spiritually-oriented
who might connect with the work and commission a piece.

The 3-message structure is intentional: message 1 earns a reply, message 2 builds interest, message 3 closes.
Pitching in message 1 skips trust-building and kills most conversations before they start.

NOTE: The psychedelic/visionary art community and festival circuit are a future opportunity, not the current focus.
Only reference that angle if Jackson explicitly brings it up.

When this skill is invoked, ask:
1. Who specifically are we reaching out to? (artist, camp leader, retreat organizer, etc.)
2. Where did Jackson find them? (Instagram, in person, mutual connection, event)
3. Does Jackson have a mutual connection or shared context to reference?
4. What piece or type of work is Jackson thinking of showing them?

Then return:

**Message 1 (the opener):**
- Goal: get a reply, not make a sale
- Lead with genuine observation about their work or world
- Reference the shared context (festival, aesthetic, community) if there is one
- End with a low-friction question or a piece of Jackson's work — not a pitch
- Under 5 lines

**Message 2 (if they reply):**
- Offer to show them something — a process video or a finished piece
- One sentence about the meaning behind the work (the "oneness" message)
- Still no price, no pressure

**Message 3 (if interest is shown):**
- Now you can talk specifics: what a commission looks like, size, timeline, price range
- Frame price as investment in something made specifically for them

Rules:
- NEVER pitch in message 1
- NEVER lead with price
- The process video is your best sales tool — show it before you say anything about cost
- $2k–$3k is the range. Don't apologize for it. The right buyer doesn't balk.
- If they say it's too expensive: "I understand — these are made one at a time and take real time.
  I'd rather make fewer pieces that matter than more that don't." Don't negotiate down unless there's a reason.
- The goal is buyers who connect with the work, not buyers who just want a deal.

**Where to find this community:**
- Instagram: visionary art accounts, Burning Man artists (#burningman, #burningmanart,
  #visionaryart, #psychedelicart, #consciousart)
- Festival artist collectives and camp accounts
- Plant medicine / retreat organizers who decorate their spaces
- Conscious entrepreneur communities (often on Instagram and Telegram)
- In person: local markets, festivals, wellness events, yoga studios, sound healing events
